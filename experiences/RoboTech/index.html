1The Challenge & Solution

Pioneering the use of low cost robots to cleanup the ocean

The Challenge

  • RoboTech gave us 36 hours to come up with an engineering solution to a current pertinent environmental climate issue
  • Microplastics became abundant and were found in human blood. We determined that oceanic microplastic poses a massive risk to our health and bio systems and we need to solve this problem.
  • Current cleanup systems are very expensive and limited in areas they can clean
  • The severity and scale of the problem call for a cheap effective large coverage solution

Our Solution

  • Mussels have an inherit natural ability to filter out microplastic from water through digestion
  • Designed a low cost robotic solar powered byou system that carries Mussels around oceans to clean out microplastics through bio functions
  • Created an prototype 3D model to prove design feasibility
  • Conducted extensive research into the physics of Mussel biofiltration, applicable scale and feasibility of operations.
  • Identified potential areas of swarm operation and robot to robot communication protocols
  • Derived a full-scale business plan with goals to expand operation to Pacific Ocean in 3 years. Identified sponsors.
